## Decisions to know (differences from the YM40 build)

- Scale anchor, chosen the same way as the YM40 question: the toothed bezel edge (tips) is the 42 mm. Silhouette radius at the tips 486.1..487.9 px (165..195 deg, centre (959.5, 1425.2)), left tip x 472.9 -> 23.171 px/mm (the YM40 had 23.0). Here the scoop gaps are opaque black in the alpha, so the outline is a circle; it is measured on the alpha, not on the scoop tips. If the designer prefers the case flank or the YM40's 23.0 camera as the 42 mm reference, only `case_edge_left_x` in the measure file changes (the model scales; kit `scale_px_per_mm` = S / 2). Lug-to-lug comes out at 49.2 mm; the crown is 4.0 mm long.
- Bezel: black ceramic insert (no platinum band). Rings: dial flat 339, crystal edge 355, bright polished inner ring 356..380 modelled as the bezel chamfer (1.04 mm), black band to 465.3, polished ring to 471, 60 scoops to 486.6 (px from the ring centre). `flute_depth` 0.05 mm (outline is a circle), `flute_phase` 0.5 (scoop at 12), 6 samples per tooth.
- Hands: same angles as the YM40 (304.8 / 63.3 / 186.0, about 10:09:31) but longer and wider: hour length 9.15 mm with the Mercedes ring (centre s 138 px, r 41), minute 13.3 mm, SILVER seconds hand (tip 14.0 mm, tail disc s -116 r 14, lollipop s 181.5 r 25). Hour hand is a threshold-110 mask trace; minute and seconds are hand-picked polygons (`shapes`) on the 3.3x rotated crops, because the minute tip overlaps the 2 o'clock index and the dark facets are below the mask threshold.
- Bracelet: all brushed titanium (no polished centre links); joint rows in `measure.bracelet_px`; column lines x 859 / 1063 (top), 858.5 / 1062.5 (bottom).
- Tracker colour check: the photo shows a black dial, black ceramic insert and titanium: matches "black / titanium". Seconds hand is silver, not cyan.

Registration checks (overlays on 2x..5x crops): hand traces on the photographed hands (hour ring, minute taper over the index, seconds needle / tail disc / lollipop); rings on the bezel edges; scoop phase at 12 and the 12 o'clock triangle; bracelet joints top and bottom; Cyclops outline.
