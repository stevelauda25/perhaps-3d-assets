Catalog 2 line: Patek Philippe, Calatrava Weekly Calendar, 5212A, 40mm, Steel, Leather strap, Brand New, 40000 / 42000 (row 82; the SKU appears once, so the published file is `models/watches/patek-5212a.glb`).
The watch shows a cream dial with a day-of-the-week ring (inner), a week-number ring and a month ring (outer), a date window at 3, gunmetal applied indices, dark gunmetal leaf hour and minute hands, a month needle to 12, a day needle with a red tip, a week needle with a red tip and a black spade-shaped hand; the strap is brown leather with cream stitching and a pink lining between the lugs.
