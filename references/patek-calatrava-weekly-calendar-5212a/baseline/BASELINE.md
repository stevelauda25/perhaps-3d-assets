Front-relief build on the 40 mm steel / cream dial (day, week-number and month rings, date window at 3) / brown leather strap catalogue render (Catalog 2 row 82). Follower of the Calatrava 6007G template ([blue baseline](../../patek-calatrava-6007g-blue/baseline/BASELINE.md)): the step `pipeline/steps/watch_patek_calatrava6007g.py` is reused read-only with no code change (spec driven); a prep copy and a polygon script are new because this photo has its own camera and a different bezel, crown, hand set and strap. Four correction passes, all emission / material tuning (the geometry matched on the first build). Approval is the designer's; this file is not a similarity score.

## Zoom check (`zoom_check.jpg`, 3x crops of the 2048 frame, reference | render)
- Sharp: hands, hub, red tips and the applied indices (faceted shading comes from the photo, edges and widths agree within 1 px); the bezel, case and lug edges; the crown outline; strap grain and stitching.
- Soft: all thin print (day, week and month rings, numerals, logo) is a little softer and lighter than the photo (the dial map is the native photo crop and is shown at 0.75 x in this frame; the render's own pixel filter adds blur; the app's native view is sharper than this comparison frame; enlargement cannot add detail the 1920 px photo does not have).
- Seen and fixed: grey blotches on the cream dial from the crystal (alpha 0.02) and the dial's diffuse lighting (base_scale 0.1 and alpha 0.005 removed them); the steel texture over 2048 px (downscaled).
- Remains: the crown cap's right end is a flat disc where the photo shows a rounded, engraved cap; the crown flutes are 14 uniform flutes, the photo has a slightly irregular pattern; no 3D relief on the rings or numerals (printed flat).

Known differences: the background, the faint drop shadow below the strap and the soft alpha rim are not part of the asset; the dial's three rings, the date window ("18") and the logo are texture only; the hands are flat extrusions with no ridge, the shading and the black / grey facets are in the projected photo; the indices have no real facets (flat slab, photographed facets); crown cap flat; the strap has no 3D emboss or stitching (flat photo projection), top corners chamfered rather than rounded; the pink lining between the lugs is the slab's flat photo (lug inner flanks not modelled); bezel and lug heights are guesses; the case slab's bottom band under the bezel (camera tilt) is slab, not bezel. Only grey luminance was compared; the hue of the red tips and the brown strap was judged by eye on the side by side, not measured. Not verified: designer approval.

## Template for further 5212A variants
Copy `config/watches/patek-calatrava5212a-5212a{,-measure}.json` and `jobs/patek-calatrava5212a-5212a.json` to the new variant key (same folder naming `references/patek-calatrava-weekly-calendar-<key>`), run the prep and polys scripts with the key, check the case circle on the new photo (a new photo from the same studio may share this camera: compare circle and pivot first), re-measure the hands in `hands5212a` of the measure file if the hand set differs, keep the step and kit preset.
