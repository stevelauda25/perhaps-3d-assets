Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.omega_diver300m_prep 21030422003001 && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/omega-diver300m-21030422003001.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/omega-diver300m-21030422003001/omega-diver300m-21030422003001.glb models/watches/omega-21030422003001.glb`.

State: as the template (steel emission now 0.30, specular 0; hands and hub `hands_photo` 0.43; crystal alpha 0.02; case outline = alpha silhouette incl. helium valve and crown shoulder), dial map emission 0.29 (blue wave dial; 0.30 gave dark 1.025 / wave 1.000 / print 0.986, 0.29 gives 1.019 / 0.996 / 0.983). What differs from the template:
- **Scale**: the 210.30 photo is the 210.32 photo scaled by 0.99577 about the pivot (left silhouette circle r 270.81 px = 42 mm, 12.8957 px/mm; ECC affine fit 0.9959..0.9962; the template's alpha scaled by 0.99577 reproduces this silhouette within 0.2 px over rows 150..780). The measure JSON is therefore the template's px values scaled about the pivot, and the mm values of rings, hands, crown, lugs and heights equal the template's. No new prep or step was needed (the prep takes the sku as argument).
- **Bracelet** (`strap.kind` "bracelet"): nine columns (outer brushed, polished strip, inner brushed, polished strip, centre brushed, polished strip, inner brushed, polished strip, outer brushed), each with its own `pieces_mm` list top and bottom: three staggered joint sets, measured per column as the centroid of the dark joint (grey < 110) from brightness scans, e.g. top outer 30.9 / 24.5 mm, inner + polished 33.9 / 29.6 / 24.3, centre 31.7 / 24.6; bottom outer 25.4 / 33.1 / 39.3, inner + polished 24.7 / 30.9 / 37.9, centre 25.5 / 33.7 / 40.2 (mm from the pivot). Eight column boundaries = the edges of the four polished strips (grey > 225, block medians along the bracelet, the bracelet tapers 0.1 to 0.2 mm per side toward the ends); column gap `groove` 0 and bevel 0.1 (the template's 0.06 gap showed black hairlines between the columns); `z_*` 3.4 outer, 3.45 inner, 3.5 polished / centre.

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, in the 2048 frame, render : photo): dial dark 1.019 (36.8 vs 36.1), dial wave grey 0.996, dial bright print / indices 0.983, bezel ceramic dark 1.047, bezel white numerals 0.998, case flank steel 0.995, lug top 1.021, lug bottom 1.005, bracelet top (outer L 1.015, polished 1.019 to 1.035, inner 1.022 / 1.024, centre 1.021, outer R 1.018), bracelet bottom (outer L 1.010, polished 1.015, inner 1.009 / 1.010, centre 1.010, outer R 1.007), crown 1.002, helium valve 1.021, hands zone 0.988, whole watch 1.012. Mean abs grey difference over the silhouette 5.4 (edge pixels, the bracelet ends, the soft alpha rim).

Known differences: the background and the soft alpha rim are not part of the asset; the dark blue ceramic reads about 1.5 grey levels light (the dark floor is base-colour lighting); the bracelet is flat pieces with the projected photo (brushing, polished strips and joint shadows are texture, no 3D relief, the polished strips are not raised); the joints are straight cuts at the measured rows (left and right outer / inner columns are placed at their own rows, but each strip of one link uses the same row, differences below 2 px are not followed); the bracelet ends are straight cuts at the alpha ends (the photo has rounded outer corners and a darker end bevel, 2 px inside the alpha end); the sliver of the outer link between the lug tip and its first joint (0.3 to 0.6 mm) and the links under the case are carried by the case slab / texture; helium valve and crown as in the template (valve a flat plate, crown cap a smooth lathe); skeleton hands are flat extrusions (photographed window and lume are texture); heights are the template's (no side view). Not verified: designer approval.
