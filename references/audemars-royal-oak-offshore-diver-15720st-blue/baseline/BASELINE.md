How the variant was registered: the blue catalogue render is the green one rendered 0.9 px right and 0.72 px higher (ECC translation fits on the hub, three hands, all eight screws, both crowns, flanks and the bezel opening agree within 0.15 px). Pivot (599.1, 795.88), octagon, opening circle, screws, crown bases and crown-replacement rows are therefore the green values + (0.9, -0.72), checked by overlay (octagon, opening, dial plate edge, screws, hand axes, lug rows all on the photo). Scale 6.9095 px/mm from the left flat of the case (plateau 454.0 over rows 790..830). Per-photo differences re-measured: the lugs of this render are about 4.3 px (top) and 4.8 px (bottom) longer relative to the pivot (tip_top 25.25 mm, tip_bottom 27.5 mm), the strap seams (x 572.5 / 627.5 top, 572.5 / 629.2 bottom, starting at rows 599.5 / 1021) and strap ends (extreme rows 31.6 / 41.6 mm, notch floors 30.8 / 41.15 mm); the rotating-ring sector is white (the green one is cream), texture only.

State: steel parts (case slab, bezel, screws, crowns) carry the reference photo projected from the front (steel emission 0.31, specular 0); the strap is the non-metallic `strap_photo` (same projection, emission 0.30, roughness 0.8; the green used 0.17: the dark blue rubber needs more emission for the same ratio); hands, hub and lume use `hands_photo` (emission 0.43); the dial is the 2x photo map (blue waffle, indices, print, black rotating ring with its white sector and the photographed hands stay in it; emission 0.30, specular 0); crystal alpha 0.02; case outline = the alpha silhouette (largest component) with the two crown rows replaced by straight lines. Geometry, heights and materials are the template's: octagon bezel with a 0.3 mm chamfer, eight screw domes, dial lathe, two hexagonal crowns with rose-gold rings, rubber strap in full / left / centre / right pieces with 0.06 mm seams. Hands hour 303.57 deg (8.39 mm), minute 48.78 deg (13.03 mm), seconds 228.7 deg (13.03 mm, lollipop at 7.27 mm; the profile lists are the green's, the hands are the same parts).
