# Baseline: AUDEMARS PIGUET Royal Oak Selfwinding 41 15500ST white (saved 2026-11-07, awaiting designer approval)

Front-relief build on the 41 mm steel / white (silver) Grande Tapisserie dial / integrated steel bracelet catalogue render (1200 x 1626 rendition), **follower of the Royal Oak 41 black template** (shared step, prep; own kit preset with the white photo's scale). The first build already registered within 0.3 px (2x frame); only emissions and the pivot / scale were adjusted (no geometry correction passes). The catalog gives the very same photo (`standup-996`) to row 89 (15510ST, "Steel, white"), so this GLB is also published under that name by the lead. Approval is the designer's; this file is not a similarity score.

Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.audemars_royaloak41_prep 15500st-white && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/audemars-royaloak41-15500st-white.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/audemars-royaloak41-15500st-white/audemars-royaloak41-15500st-white.glb models/watches/audemars-15500st-white.glb`.

State: as the black template (steel parts, hands and hub carry the photo projected from the front; dial = 2x photo map with the photographed hands; crystal alpha 0.02; one hexagonal crown; integrated bracelet from the measured seams and bars), with: dial emission 0.34 (black: 0.30), steel emission 0.30 (black: 0.31), hands emission 0.45. Scale: left flat anchored at 20.5 mm = 6.9473 px/mm (the white photo is 0.9 % narrower than the black one; vertical scale equal within 0.2 %), pivot (600.42, 792.45).

Registration: the black measure values were carried onto this photo by an ECC affine fit (steel parts' gradients, dial excluded; x 0.9911, y 0.9981) and verified on the first render by phase correlation of photo vs render patches (2x px): 8 screws within 0.26, hub 0.27, top / bottom flats 0.07 / 0.21, right flat 0.68, crown 0.08, bracelet patches 0.01..0.25; silhouette edges within 1 px at 2x (rows 240..1200). Hand tips (hour, minute, seconds) and the hub coincide in 5x zooms; the light-on-light hands could not be thresholded, so no new brightness profiles were taken (hand angles 303.8 / 49.9 / 227.3 deg, lengths 8.95 / 13.70 / 14.44 mm, widths of the black template).

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1280 frame, render : photo): whole watch 1.016, dial plate (r < 200 px) 1.001 (222.3 vs 222.0), dial bright features (photo > 150) 0.999, dial dark areas (photo <= 150, small area, minute track and hand shadows) 1.108, bezel face 1.019, bracelet top (photo rows 588..620) 1.024, bracelet bottom (985..1060) 0.992, case top (632..648) 1.037, case bottom (946..972) 0.978, crown 1.006. Mean abs grey difference over the silhouette 7.5. Emission trials, dial: 0.30 plate 0.985 / 0.31 0.989 / 0.32 0.993 / 0.34 1.001 (kept, lowest mean abs difference 7.72 at that step) / 0.37 1.013; steel 0.31 -> 0.30 improved the mean abs difference 7.72 -> 7.50 (the response is weak: 0.01 = 0.4 %; case top and bottom still split by slope shading, one value for all steel).

Known differences: as the black baseline (background / alpha rim not part of the asset; Grande Tapisserie waffle, baton indices, print, date window, minute track, facets and inlay of the hands, screw slots, brushed / polished finishes are texture only; bracelet seams are open gaps over a dark backing, so the render's seams read slightly heavier; rounded pin-link ends are flat texture on continuous bars; bracelet ends about 2 px short and square; crown without base gap line; 7 px/mm source, the 2x copy adds no detail; heights have no side view, back flat; silhouette includes about 2 px of catalogue-render tilt parallax). White-dial specific: the 3D hour and minute hands are slightly brighter / puffier than the photographed ones beside the indices (small halo on the 10 o'clock baton side), dark areas 11 % light (very few pixels), the right octagon flat is read symmetrically to the left one and sits 0.3 px off in the phase correlation.
