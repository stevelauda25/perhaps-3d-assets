## Template for the next steel-mesh / column-bracelet Breitling variants

Follow `../../breitling-superocean-42-a17375211b1s1/baseline/BASELINE.md` (Breitling template) with this step instead of `watch_breitling_superocean42` when the strap is a bracelet: `strap.kind` "bracelet", `strap.bracelet.columns` (names, any order), per column optional `x_mm[name][top|bottom]` = [[|y| mm, left x, right x], ...] (otherwise the silhouette outline, rows nearer than `edge_from` clamped), `pieces_mm[name][top|bottom]` = [[y0, y1], ...], `z_<name>`, `gap`, `lift`, `bevel`, `backing_z`. For a jubilee / oyster style bracelet use several side-by-side columns with their `x_mm` tables from joint scans (see the Black Bay 58 spec for the pattern). Commands as in the Rebuild line with a new copy of the prep (`MODEL` ref / name) and new measure / spec / job / preset files (preset: scale = 2 x px_per_mm only). Re-measure per photo: scale from the widest part of the case (here the tooth tips), pivot from the hand centre lines, ring centre from a circle fit to a ring edge, ring radii from radial scans, hands in rotated strips (angle from centre-line offsets), crown box, lug rows, bracelet pieces.
