Catalog 2 line: Patek Philippe, Calatrava, 6007G, 40mm, White gold, blue, Leather strap, Brand New, 34000 / 35700. Row 81 is the same SKU 6007G "White gold, red" with the photo `.../5720-51887?format=png` (the red variant is built from this build's template; published file names carry the variant: `patek-6007g-blue.glb`, `patek-6007g-red.glb`).
The "blue" dial of row 80 reads as a black dial with a light-blue minute track, blue guilloche-ring markers and a blue central seconds hand; the strap is black embossed leather with blue stitching and a blue lining.
