## Template for the next Datejust 41 variants (126331 Everose Rolesor / Jubilee slate Roman, 126333 Rolesor yellow / Jubilee slate)

Photos are already in place (untracked): `references/rolex-datejust-41-126331/rolex-126331_original.png` and `references/rolex-datejust-41-126333/rolex-126333_original.png` (1920 x 2857 RGBA). Checked here against the 126334 photo: both have the same alpha extent at the pivot row (x 505..1473) and the head silhouette (rows 905..1980) differs by only 271 px (the bracelets differ by a few thousand px: Jubilee edges), i.e. one camera, one case, one bezel: the head geometry (case, flutes, crown, lens position, hub) holds, the flute zone's mean abs grey difference is about 5 (metal colour, reflections). What changes: the dial (slate, applied Roman numerals: texture only; no baton indices, the Roman XII etc. come with the dial map), the bezel / centre-link metal colour (photo texture: Everose, yellow gold; two-tone Rolesor = steel case flank + gold bezel and gold centre links) and the Jubilee bracelet (five-piece). Reuse the step, prep and kit preset READ-ONLY.

**Per variant (re-measure, never trust the 126334 numbers):**
1. `measure.photo` and the ids in spec / job (the sed above); the measure `note` text.
2. `hands`: the render time is 10:09:30 on all Rolex renders, so the angles hold. The prep's colour test (`hand_ratio` 0.87: neutral pixels vs a green dial) does NOT work on a slate / silver / white dial: set `hand_ratio` to null and give grey `thr` per hand as in `rolex_submariner41_prep.py` (e.g. 70..130 on a slate dial; check the traced polygons by overlay, 3x crops), or keep the colour test if the dial has a clearly coloured hue (an Everose / yellow Rolesor slate dial is grey: use thr). The Datejust hands may be Dauphine / baton: re-trace the ROIs from the photo (hour / minute widths and tips), keep the ROI polygons wider than the hand where it touches an index / numeral.
3. `bezel`: the flute geometry (74 flutes, long parity, radii) is the same case: verify the zig-zag phase by the 3x crop at 9 o'clock (a long boundary lies on the pivot row). Gold / Everose: the metal colour is texture, only emissions may need +-0.02 (tune `steel.emission` and optionally `steel.bezel_emission` by region means: flutes, flange, chamfer, case flank, bracelet columns).
4. Dial emission (`dial.emission`, `lens.emission`): the response is weak and nonlinear; tune so the dial plate mean lands within ~1-2 % (slate is darker / flatter than the mint sunburst: start at 0.30, check centre vs rim and the bright Roman numerals separately).
5. **Jubilee bracelet** (126331, 126333; a five-piece bracelet with brushed outer links, polished semi-links and a polished centre link): the step's bracelet builder already takes any column list: in the spec set `strap.bracelet.columns` to `["outer", "semi", "centre", "semi", "outer"]` (kinds are free names), `bounds_x_mm.top / bottom` to rows of `[|y|, x1, x2, x3, x4]` (four vertical joint lines per row, measured as the gradient maxima of the vertical lines on row bands in 3x crops: the Jubilee's lines are narrower, about 2.5 mm pitch for the semi-links, centre 4 mm; `bounds` is linear in |y| between the listed rows, so a tapering bracelet needs one row near the lugs and one near the end), `pieces_mm` to one `top` / `bottom` chain of `[y0, y1]` rows per kind (the links of a Jubilee are short, about 5-6 mm each and OFFSET between neighbouring columns: read the horizontal joints for each column separately, top and bottom, in a 3x strip crop as done here: `config/watches/rolex-datejust41-126334-measure.json` `bracelet_px` lists the method), and add `z_semi` (and `z_outer`, `z_centre`) next to the other `z_*` values (the step reads `z_<kind>` per piece). Rows in px -> mm: top = (1424 - row) / 22.2146, bottom = (row - 1424) / 22.2146; the first piece of the centre / semi columns starts at 18.0 (hidden under the case). The brushed / polished texture comes from the photo.
6. **Two-tone metal** (126333 yellow Rolesor: yellow-gold bezel and centre links; 126331 Everose Rolesor): nothing structural: the steel map is one photo projection, so the gold bezel / links are in it. Check each region's emission by means and keep the steel emission uniform unless the gold regions read more than ~3 % off (then `steel.bezel_emission` for the bezel, a second `bracelet_photo` copy in a COPY of the step only if the gold links need their own).
7. `sources.md` row text and the dial / bracelet description in `REFERENCE_PLAN.md`; if the photo's dial colour is not the tracker's variant, say so in the report.
