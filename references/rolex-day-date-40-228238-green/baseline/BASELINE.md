Approval is the designer's; this file is not a similarity score and says nothing about photorealism.

Known differences: the background and alpha rim are not part of the asset; the dial print (the green ombre gradient is the photo map itself, so the fade is exact, but dark areas read about 5 % light: a floor of about 2 grey levels from the diffuse base), Roman numerals and indices, day window, date numeral, rehaut engraving, hand faces and the bracelet grain are texture only; the 3D hands, bezel facets (152 real facets), crown, crystal, dial plane, Cyclops, links and case slab are geometry. The raised camera makes the flange / bezel circles non-concentric with the dial, so the 3D facets' zig-zag edges sit up to about 8 px (0.2 mm) off the photo's facets (invisible in the front view because every steel part shows the projected photo); the facets read slightly lower in contrast than the photo (left facets std 9.8 vs 11.6 grey); thin seam lines show between the centre-link pieces where the photo has smooth shading; the minute hand mask includes a slice of the seconds hand near the hub (same photo, no visible effect); outer links are 0.5 mm sine arches, centre links flat; no lume; heights have no side view, back flat. The tracker dial colour "green ombre Roman" matches the photo (dark green ombre, Roman numerals X / III / IV-VI clusters plus batons).

## Zoom check

`zoom_check.jpg` (3x crops of the 1600 frames, reference | render): upper dial (coronet, ROLEX / OYSTER PERPETUAL / DAY-DATE, THURSDAY), hands and hub, lower dial / 6 o'clock. Sharp: the hub cap and screw, the hour / minute / seconds hand edges (gold face, dark facet and both edges line up with the photo within about 1 px; flat tips, tails and the seconds counterweight match), the applied indices and numerals (VI, batons), the SUPERLATIVE CHRONOMETER lines and SWISS MADE, the coronet. Soft: the dial print is exactly as soft as the retailer's upscaled photo (a touch softer than the INTER_AREA reference where the render's filter takes 1 px lines; the shipped dial map is native 36 px/mm, the steel map 0.80 of native); bright gold highlights clip a little (flank 1.9 % light). No halos, dark gaps, stair-steps or misplaced hand edges were visible in the crops; the thin seam lines between the top centre links are the only added artefact (visible at 2.4x, not in the 3x dial crops).
