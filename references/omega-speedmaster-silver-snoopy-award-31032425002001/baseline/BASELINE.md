Known differences: the background and the soft alpha rim are not part of the asset; the nylon weave, the stitching along both edges and the shading at the lugs are flat photo texture on a flat slab (no relief, no keepers in this render); the strap ends are the 0.25 mm sampled alpha edge (the arch of the photo reads a little more angular and its corners can show a hairline of the grey map margin); the strap end at the case and the pockets next to the bezel are an exact cut-out, the strap end itself is a straight edge (the photo has a slightly frayed, shadowed edge); the case and the twisted lugs are flat plates carrying the photo, the crown barrel end is square, the pushers are plain cylinders with a rounded end face (their photographed dark bands are texture; the render pusher is about 1 to 2 px longer at the corners; a faint vertical shading band shows between barrel and cap); the sub-dial and Snoopy engraving, indices, print and the Ag925 mark are the dial photo (2x enlargement of a 1024 px source, so softer than the Tudor builds); dark blue regions (sub-dials, bezel, hands) read 5 to 8 percent light; the chrono seconds needle and the hour / minute hands are 3D relief over the photographed hands, edges can differ by about 0.5 to 1 px; heights are copied from the Moonwatch build (no side view). ## Template for the next nylon-strap Moonwatch variants

Copy the prep (`pipeline/omega_snoopy_prep.py`, edit `MODEL` and the default sku), the step is reusable as is (`watch_omega_snoopy`): per variant a measure / spec / job JSON, a kit preset, and the numbers of the strap block (`strap.nylon.pieces_mm`, `edge_from`, emission) plus `case.notch` when the photo shows a gap between the strap end and the case. Commands as in the Moonwatch template, with `omega-snoopy` as the pattern.
