## Verified specification (Omega product page)

42 mm asymmetrical steel case; thickness 13.54 mm; lug-to-lug 47.50 mm; lug width 20 mm; Hesalite crystal with OMEGA logo in the centre; anodised aluminium bezel ring with dot over 90; embossed Seahorse medallion caseback; black step dial; brushed steel bracelet, five arched links per row; foldover clasp with comfort setting; 5 bar. Hand-wound calibre 3861 (third-party sources; not visible externally).
