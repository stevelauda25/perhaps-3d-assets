## Visual analysis

Seq1/06: blue perimeter, fine white inset outline; white CASIO at upper left, yellow italic F-91W at upper right; yellow ALARM CHRONOGRAPH; red arrows and WR; silver-gray green LCD with black non-emissive segmented numerals. Resin crystal is a separately manufactured transparent component, not mineral glass. Outer case has tapered lugs and chamfered shoulders; stepped inclined bezel surrounding inset crystal.

02: shallow 8.5 mm construction, underside stamped metal back extending fractionally past case shell. One right lower pusher, two left pushers; small transverse strap pins at lug ends.

03: brushed metal caseback with circular pressed relief and four corner screws. Black resin buckle, black tongue, sliding keeper. Twelve rounded elongated strap holes and thin parallel longitudinal channels. No serial numbers invented.

05: band narrows from case shoulders to buckle/tip. Three horizontal relief lines on either side near case, elongated centers. Short buckle half and longer perforated half.

kv, wrist and Seq2: contextual perspectives verify natural flexible strap curvature.

## Modeling uncertainty

Only overall case dimensions are factory-specified. Subcomponent dimensions and band lengths are proportionally reconstructed from the official photographs. Rear micro-engraving is not fully legible and is not fabricated; no serial number or country-of-origin claim is added.
