## Template for the next Offshore Diver variants

The blue Offshore Diver (catalog row 78, same SKU 15720ST, photo `standup-1339`) follows with these files: the prep, step and kit preset are generic; the variant needs only a new measure / spec / job JSON. The variant key is `15720st-blue` (the SKU repeats, so the key carries the colour, also in the GLB name). The lead has to download the blue photo as `references/audemars-royal-oak-offshore-diver-15720st-blue/audemars-15720st-blue_original.png` and `..._1200x1626.png` first.

```sh
key=15720st-blue                                                          # variant key; the green one is 15720st-green
ref=references/audemars-royal-oak-offshore-diver-$key                     # holds audemars-$key_original.png and audemars-$key_1200x1626.png
python3 - <<'PY'   # measure file: copy, then MEASURE every value again on the new photo (list below)
import shutil; shutil.copy("config/watches/audemars-offshorediver42-15720st-green-measure.json", "config/watches/audemars-offshorediver42-15720st-blue-measure.json")
PY
python3 -B -m pipeline.audemars_offshorediver42_prep $key                 # 2x copy, trace, dial_photo.jpg, steel_photo.jpg (MODEL constants stay: same ref prefix, name, 42 mm)
cp config/watches/audemars-offshorediver42-15720st-green.json config/watches/audemars-offshorediver42-$key.json   # change model, trace, dial_map, steel_map, hands (profile / lume), emissions, strap seams / pieces
cp jobs/audemars-offshorediver42-15720st-green.json jobs/audemars-offshorediver42-$key.json                       # replace the id / output / spec / trace / glb / reference strings (sed '15720st-green' -> '15720st-blue'); step and kit preset stay
cp references/audemars-royal-oak-offshore-diver-15720st-green/REFERENCE_PLAN.md $ref/REFERENCE_PLAN.md          # edit the measured values and the catalog row
python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs validate jobs/audemars-offshorediver42-$key.json && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/audemars-offshorediver42-$key.json --force
cp outputs/jobs/audemars-offshorediver42-$key/audemars-offshorediver42-$key.glb models/watches/audemars-$key.glb
```

The kit preset `config/presets/watch_kit_white_front_audemars_offshorediver42.json` is shared as it is while the new photo has the same scale (6.9143 px/mm) and pivot (598.2, 796.6); if the prep prints another `scale_px_per_mm`, copy the preset and set `scale_px_per_mm` = 2 x that and `center_px` = [640, 640] (1280 frame), and point the job's kit stage at the copy.

**Template (keep as is):** the step `pipeline/steps/watch_audemars_offshorediver42.py` (case slab from the silhouette, octagon bezel ring by rays with chamfer, eight screws, dial lathe with the photo map, crystal, hands from profile / lume lists, hexagonal crowns from `trace.crowns`, rubber strap with full / left / centre / right pieces), the prep, the kit lighting / camera, materials (`steel_gap`, `lume`, `crystal`), pose and depth scale, heights (`case` body_z 6.6 / tip_z 3.4 / bezel_z 8.9 / chamfer_z 8.2 / chamfer_mm 0.3 / bezel_bevel 0.2, `crystal`, `dial` z 7.0 / ring_z 7.5, `hands.z`, `crown.z` 5.0), steel emission 0.31, hands emission 0.43, dial emission 0.30 (check a lighter or bluer dial both ways), strap emission 0.17 and roughness 0.8 (check the blue rubber: the response is nonlinear and the colour changes the ratio), strap `gap` 0.06, `z_side` = `z_centre` 3.3, `lift`, bevels, the comparison-script conventions (continuous px, the 2x pivot moved to the frame centre).

**Per variant (re-measure on the photo, never copy):**
- Photo: AP serves a 400 x 542 PNG; the working source is the `&wid=1200&hei=1626` rendition (same watch, 344 x 506 px of it). The two photos of the SAME model may differ by a pixel or two in pivot and by about 1 px in the flats (tilt); do not assume the green values.
- `pivot`: intersect the centre lines of the hour and minute hands (PCA of the hand mask `R > G + 6 or R > 190` restricted to a strip along the hand; a blue dial needs another mask: the rose-gold hands and white lume still separate by R > B + something, check on the photo) and check against the hub circle fit.
- `anchor.rows`: rows where the left flat of the case is straightest (here 780..815): the median alpha edge = 21 mm from the pivot; verify that the printed `scale_px_per_mm` is near 6.91 (a different value means the pivot or the flat was misread). Also check the scale against the lug-to-lug / screw spacing (screws on the vertex rays at 134.7 px here).
- `octagon`: left / right flats from the silhouette, top / bottom flats from the polished chamfer lines at x = pivot (here 650.3 / 946.0); `opening_circle_px`: radial scans of the black ring outer edge (fit on the angles where the ring is dark, here 109.5 px about (598.8, 795.1)); `dial_flat_r_px` / `ring_in_r_px`: end of the printed plate / inner edge of the ring (~90 / 91 px).
- `screws_px` and `screw_r_px`: read the eight centres on 8x tiles (hexagon heads; the slots have different angles per screw: ignore them).
- `crowns`: base point on the axis at the flank (3 o'clock: (741.0, 803.0), 90 deg; 10 o'clock: (483.1, 719.75), 300 deg), hex length / circumradius, ring length / radius; `crown_replace`: straight lines that replace the silhouette rows of the crowns (right: the guard notch wall; left: the line from the flank above the crown to the flank below it; start the left line at the first row where the crown begins, otherwise a spike remains).
- Hands: angle and length (clockwise from 12) from the mask, half-width profiles (mm along the hand, tail to tip) and lume bars in the spec; the seconds lollipop circle (centre s 50.3, r 6.4 px for the green). The variant's hands are the same rose-gold parts: check them with the render-versus-photo mask offset (should be below 0.2 px).
- Strap: `tip_rows_px` (lug top 626 / bottom 981 px here; tip_top + tip_bottom a multiple of 0.25 mm), seams `centre_x_mm` (x 571.5 / 626.5 top, 571.5 / 627.5 bottom here), `pieces_mm` (full piece to where the seams start: top y 604, bottom y 1015; side / centre pieces start 0.6 mm earlier, end at the strap end rows 31.0 / 40.75 mm and the centre top at the notch floor 30.25 mm), `backing_to_mm.top` (stop the dark backing before the end notch), strap `emission`.
- Emissions: region ratios with the list above (dial plate / bright / dark, black ring, bezel face, strap top / bottom, lugs, crowns, whole watch); the blue dial and the blue rubber are the two things that differ in colour: re-tune dial and strap emission by two runs each.
