What differs from the silver template (all in the measure / spec JSON, no code):
- **Crop and scale**: the photo is the same render at a 3.6 percent larger scale and another offset. The eight bezel screws, template matched against the silver photo, give an isotropic scale of 1.036 and offset (-22.4, -22.0) (residual under 0.7 px); the silver traced bezel-outline quarter, the screws and the guard line mapped through it fit the blue photo within about 1 px at all four corners (gridded 4x crops). The WSSA0018 kit preset and its (+49.0, +69.6) translation therefore do not apply; own preset 15.930 px/mm, translation (+69.8, +89.8).
- **Mask**: tolerance 5, not 6. At 6 the flood fill leaks into the bright top bracelet edge on this photo (the corner background is (236,240,241), slightly different from the silver photo's) and removes rows 81..200 of the strap (5 to 6 differs by 5,071 px, all at the top bracelet; 4 to 5 by 394 px = halo). A 1 px light halo remains around the crown dome.
- **Measured per photo**: pivot (364.6, 354.6) = intersection of the three hand centre lines (residual under 0.1 px; the hub ring's circle fit centres 1 px lower, the ring is shaded), hub radius 9.3 px; opening x 253.0..476.5, y 242.5..462.0, dial rim x 259.5..475.0, y 250.5..458.5 (the mapped silver values were up to 8 px off on the top and bottom edges: this variant's chamfer differs); crown octagon x 518.5..543, half height 25.5 px, axis y 356.0, cabochon to 556.2, guard x 541.5; end-link edges x 277.8 / 452.2 top, 278.0 / 453.0 bottom; bracelet joints top 199.5 / 160.4 / 129.8 / 104.9 / 87.8 (end 81.5), bottom 511.0 / 550.0 / 589.0 / 623.2 / 649.5 (end 663.5) (the lower pieces are shorter than the mapped silver ones: 25 and 21 px against 34 and 21).
- **Hands** (about 10:09, steel sword hands, not blue): hour 7.96 mm at 305.5 deg, minute 11.51 mm at 49.9 deg, seconds 11.63 mm at 215.1 deg with a 4.95 mm tail at 35.3 deg; the blades are wider than the silver spec's (hour half width 0.34 to 0.68 mm, minute 0.15 to 0.40 mm) and are listed in the spec's `hands.profile` (measured from grey-level profiles in the hand frame). The three hands are shorter than the silver ones (7.96 / 11.51 / 11.63 against 8.58 / 12.17 / 12.23 mm).
- **Emissions** (render : photo): steel 0.27 unchanged; dial 0.36 -> 1.029, 0.35 -> 1.025, 0.32 -> 1.011 (the response is flat; the dial is 64 percent dark pixels that always read about 3 grey levels light, so the dial mean is matched against the bright parts: 0.32 leaves bright 0.969, 0.36 had 0.981 and dark 1.106); hands 0.30 -> 0.988, 0.31 -> 0.992.

Silhouette: the render is on white, so near-white highlights of the strap and bezel cannot be separated from the background by a threshold (8,800 of 588,158 mask px differ, mostly those highlights); the outline is the mask by construction and was checked on the gridded overlays.

Known differences: drop shadow and background are not part of the asset; dark dial areas read about 2 to 3 grey levels lighter than the photo while bright features read 3 percent darker (same behaviour as the silver build, mirrored by the dial emission trade-off); the lower right of the watch (right flank, bottom-right horn, lower bracelet) is 1 to 3.5 percent darker and the upper left 0 to 1 percent brighter (one emission value per material, the slanted flank ring and the card lighting add a small gradient); the hands' 3D blades are symmetric about the axis through the pivot while the photographed blades are lit asymmetrically (a bright facet and a darker facet; the three hand centre lines meet at the pivot, but the hour blade's body centre line is 2 px off the pivot-to-tip line, so about 1 px of the 3D outline can show the dark dial pixels at an edge); the seconds hand's tip merges with the VII numeral, so its length (11.63 mm) is where the thin line stops; the hub is a lathe ball (rings come from the photo); the bracelet links are flat slabs with a small edge radius (rounded section and chamfers are photographed shading only); the flank is a sloping ring so the brushed horns' roundness is photographed shading; the screw heads are shallow domes (slots are texture); the crown is a lathe (facets and the blue cabochon come from the photo; guard pockets are filled by the flank ring); the printed dial (Roman numerals, minute track, date window 18, CARTIER / AUTOMATIC / SWISS MADE) is one flat photo map; the whole watch is a 2x upscale of a 750 px photo (no recovered detail); heights are estimated (no side view, back flat); the case is 4 percent taller than the listed lug-to-lug against its width.
