State: as the green template: steel parts carry the photo projected from the front (`steel_photo`, specular 0, roughness 0.6, emission 0.29); hands and hub emission 0.36 over the photographed hands that stay in the dial map; dial plate = native photo map, emission 0.31 (unchanged from green), specular 0; crystal alpha 0.02; dark non-photo bracelet backing. No baked shadows beyond the photo, no specular. Registration verified by render-vs-photo overlay: silhouette IoU 0.994 (flood fill from the frame), identical to the green build. The hands (time about 10:09:30, as green) line up in the 4x crop; no hand correction needed.

Dial colour: the photo shows a light champagne-yellow dial (mean RGB about 211 / 190 / 143 in the 1280 frame, hue roughly 40 deg, soft low-contrast grain), i.e. a golden champagne rather than a grey or pinkish beige. Tracker variant is "beige" (the lead matched it to file suffix 0007); the image was not substituted. Designer decision whether the tracker label should read champagne / yellow.

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1280 frame, render : photo; masks: dial disc r < 165 px about the ring centre, hands and print included): whole watch 1.004, dial plate 0.997 (188.3 vs 187.8 grey levels), dial centre r<60 0.998, mid 60-110 0.998, rim 110-165 0.996, dial bright features (grey > 200) 0.990, rehaut ring 0.991, bezel face 1.023, bracelet top 1.019, bracelet bottom 1.016, case top lug 1.017, crown 1.036. The steel ratios equal the green template's under the same masks (bracelet top 1.018, bottom 1.016, bezel 1.022, crown 1.036), as the steel pixels are identical; they read 1.5-2 % light here because these masks include bright polished edges (the template's own table used other masks, whole 1.002); steel emission left at the template's 0.29. Mean abs grey difference over the silhouette 6.1 (1 px print / grain edges).

Known differences: as the green baseline (background and alpha rim not part of the asset; print, indices, lume strips, hand facets, bezel reflections, bracelet grain are texture; 3D hands, crown, dome, crystal, dial plane, links, case slab are geometry; no lume geometry; end links ramp straight; no side view, back flat). The black print and hand facets render a little greyer than the photo (pixels darker than 20 grey in the photo average 4.5 vs 19 in the render, because the render's pixel filter is softer than the INTER_AREA reference around 1 px strokes); more visible on this light dial than on the dark ones.
