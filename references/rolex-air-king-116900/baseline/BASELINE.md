Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.rolex_airking_prep 116900 && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/rolex-airking-116900.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/rolex-airking-116900/rolex-airking-116900.glb models/watches/rolex-116900.glb`.

State: same recipe as 126900. Steel parts (case, bezel, crown, bracelet backing) carry the photo projected from the front (steel_photo, specular 0, roughness 0.6, emission 0.285); bracelet rows / centre pieces the same photo (top 0.275, bottom 0.29); three hands = traced photo outlines (flat polygons at z 7.4 / 7.65 / 7.9, thickness 0.12, depth scale 0.35; hands_photo, emission 0.40); dial plate = native photo map, emission 0.30, specular 0; crystal alpha 0.02. Emission values were carried over from 126900 unchanged and landed within the ratios below. Measured here: scale 32.6253 px/mm (left flank 784.3 to pivot 1437.0 = 20.0 mm), pivot (1437.0, 1233.0), bezel outer ellipse half axes 653.0 x 635.2 px (20.0 x 19.5 mm), window edge circle r 553.7 px (16.97 mm, std 5.8 px because the edge is soft), hands hour 305 deg (leaf head, tip 7.9 mm) / minute 63.4 deg (tip 13.6 mm) / seconds 185.25 deg (line 16 px wide, lume disc r 24 px at 7.8 mm, tail disc r 14 px at -4.6 mm, tip 14.3 mm), hub 30 / 20 px, crown barrel axis y 1247.5, radius up to 111 px, axial 104 px, lug tips 22.75 / 25.25 mm.

Differs from 126900: the hour hand has a lume leaf head (no Mercedes disc) and the minute hand a spear tip; the silhouette has no separate crown guards (the notch line x 2080 replaces the barrel rows 1137..1357); the lugs and the Oyster bracelet are the same design, but the joints and centre-link pieces were re-measured (9 centre pieces, 3 + 3 outer rows); the source is a different render (different camera, scale 1.444x, soft) so the comparison frame is 1536 px.

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1536 frame, render : photo): whole watch 0.998, dial plate 1.009 (65.4 vs 64.8), dial bright features 0.996, dial dark areas 1.051, rehaut ring 0.962, bezel face 1.010, bracelet top centre / outer 1.011 / 1.005, bracelet bottom centre / outer 1.003 / 1.003, lug top L / R 1.012 / 1.012, lug bottom L / R 0.999 / 0.994, crown 0.994. Mean abs grey difference over the photo silhouette 2.9; silhouette IoU 0.957 ("render not near-white" test; the photo's white bezel highlights read as background).

Known differences: the background and the alpha rim (a 1 px light/dark rim along the silhouette, strongest along the right bezel edge above the crown) are not part of the asset; dial print, numerals, minute track, rehaut, brushed grain, bezel highlights and the black reflection at the top, crown flutes and the lume are texture only; hands, crown, bezel, dial plane, bracelet rows and centre links are geometry; hand outlines were traced at the 50 % level of a soft photo (edge uncertainty about 2 px, the minute spear tip and the upper edge of the hour head are partly hidden by the "10" numeral and the OYSTER PERPETUAL print, so those two stretches are reconstructed); the seconds line is 16 px wide (blur included, so it may read slightly wide at zoom); the rehaut ring reads 4 % dark and the dark dial areas 5 % light (as in 126900); the hub shows a small bright highlight at its upper left that the photo has less of; the case tips slope to the bracelet by a straight ramp; the centre-link pieces are flat-topped rounded rectangles; hands flat; no side view, back flat. The retailer's JPEG/upscale softness is carried into the dial map and the hand textures: nothing is sharper than the source.

## Zoom check

`zoom_check.jpg` (3x crops, reference | render; the tool centres on the non-white bounding box, so its crops show upper dial / hub area, hands and hub, and the lower dial). Sharp: nothing is sharp in an absolute sense; the render is exactly as soft as the photo (both about 8 to 10 px edge width at the crop size). Lines up within about 1 px at 3x: ROLEX / crown print, the hour hand leaf head and baton, the minute baton and tip, the seconds line, tail disc, lume disc, the 6 numeral, bezel edge. Soft: all print (the Air-King script and the small SUPERLATIVE CHRONOMETER lines are readable only as in the photo), the minute track, brushed grain (the source's softness, not a render artifact). Checked with extra 4x crops and difference images: the crown (bezel edge touching it shows a 1 px bright rim in the difference), the lower seconds ring. Visible residue: the hub highlight and the faint 1 px outlines around hands in the difference image; no halos, dark gaps, stair-steps or misplaced hand edges are visible.

## Template for the next Air-King / 40 mm Oyster-case variants

This follower used the 126900 template unchanged; see `../../rolex-air-king-126900/baseline/BASELINE.md` for the commands. Per-photo measurements are the same list; for a photo with a different scale use a kit preset with `scale_px_per_mm = trace scale / 2` and a frame size large enough for the whole watch (half-size of the longest extent from the pivot: here 44.1 mm * 16.31 = 719 px, so 1536).
