## Oysterflex strap recipe (for the 126519LN follower)
The photo shows NOT a flat strap: flared outer rubber sides (x 732..1188 px at the lugs, end rows 685 top / 2514 bottom with rounded corners) and a raised centre blade x 858..1060 px that ends 15 px (top, row 670.4) and 14 px (bottom, row 2528.4) beyond the sides. So the single-column layout (`columns: ["strap"]`) was NOT used: the step's edges come from the silhouette at 0.25 mm rows and cannot step at the centre-blade ends. Used the step's existing three-column layout with rubber heights:
- `strap.bracelet.columns: ["outer", "centre", "outer"]`, `z_outer` 2.8, `z_centre` 3.0, `z0` 0.6, `bevel` 0.15, `gap` 0.015 (the template's 0.06 shows a dark hairline at the column joints that the photo does not have), `inset` / `backing` unchanged.
- `bounds_x_mm` = the two centre-blade edges (gradient maxima on 20..100 row bands, x 857.3..858.1 / 1060.0..1060.7 top, 856.3..858.1 / 1059.3..1061.5 bottom), `pieces_mm`: one piece per column and side. Outer top [21.5, 32.90], bottom [23.7, 48.40]; centre top [18.0, 33.63], bottom [18.0, 49.15] (mm from the pivot; row = 1425 -/+ y x 22.4625). Start the outer pieces UNDER the case slab (21.5 / 23.7, not at the tip rows 22.93 / 25.15): starting at the tip left a one-pixel light seam where the slab edge meets the strap.
- Outer ends must stay inside the 0.25 mm silhouette quantisation: `edges(y)` rounds y to the nearest 0.25, so an outer piece may end only where the rounded row still has the full width (top end y <= 32.87, bottom y <= 48.37 here: about 1 and 2 px short of the true ends 685 / 2514). For the 126519LN check the end rows first (`fg` row widths near 670..690 and 2510..2530).
- Strap emission needs no separate value: steel 0.29 gave strap regions 1.00 to 1.02.

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1280 frame, render : photo): whole watch 1.014, dial plate (hands included) 1.014, rehaut ring 1.003, bezel chamfer 1.034, bezel 1.007, strap top centre 1.018, top left 1.016, top right 1.016, strap bottom centre 1.003, bottom left 1.012, bottom right 1.044 (the dark right side, 37 vs 38.5 grey), lug top 1.064, lug bottom 0.966, crown 1.015, case flank 1.096 (837 px edge sliver, not a fair mean). Per channel (B / G / R) dial 1.017 / 1.015 / 1.012 (champagne hue holds). Mean abs grey difference over the photo silhouette 6.0. Silhouette IoU 0.995 (727 px render-only, 751 photo-only).

Known differences: the background and alpha rim are not part of the asset; dial print, indices, sub-dial rings, the bezel scale and reflection, hand facets / lume / shadows, crown and pusher flute shading, the strap's matte grain and its soft shading gradients are texture only; geometry: six flat hands, 16 coarse flutes on crown and pushers, bezel rings, crystal, dial plane, case slab and the flat rubber columns (no curvature at the strap ends; the render's strap ends are square-cornered where the photo's outer sides have ~15 px rounded corners, and the outer ends are 1 to 2 px short). A faint vertical line at the centre blade's edges and a faint horizontal transition under the lugs remain (soft, low contrast). Dark printed areas are lighter than the photo (kit floor), the lug bottom is 3 % dark, the lug top 6 % light. No side view: heights copied from the template, back flat. Not verified: designer approval, app lighting.
