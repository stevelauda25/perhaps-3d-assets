# Omega fold-over clasp (bracelet end)

Designer-supplied references (2026-11-03) for the strap-end clasp of the Speedmaster bracelet. They show the same
clasp on another model of the family in Sedna gold; only the colour differs, the form is the reference.

- `clasp-top.webp`: closed, from above: brushed cover the bracelet's width, six polished grooves along it, raised
  polished Omega on the end, oval push buttons on both flanks about a quarter of the length from that end.
- `clasp-open-side.webp`: open, three-quarter: cover thickness, curved length, push button, blades.
- `clasp-open-inside.webp`: open, inside: blades, end barrels with slotted screws, PUSH tab.

Used by `fold_clasp` in `pipeline/steps/watch_speedmaster_v3.py` (config `speedmaster-moonwatch-black-v13-worn-clasp.json`).
