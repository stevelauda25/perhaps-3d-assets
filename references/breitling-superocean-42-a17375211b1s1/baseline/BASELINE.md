## Template for the next Breitling variants

Superocean Heritage 42 (A1032..), Chronomat B01 42 (AB0134101B1A1) and Navitimer B01 Chronograph 41 (AB0139211B1P1) follow with these files; the prep, step and kit preset are generic enough that a variant normally needs only new measure / spec / job JSON.

```sh
sku=<lower-case sku, letters and digits only>            # e.g. ab0134101b1a1
ref=references/breitling-<model-slug>-$sku                  # the folder that already holds breitling-$sku_original.webp
cp pipeline/breitling_superocean42_prep.py pipeline/breitling_<model>_prep.py     # edit MODEL (ref, name, case_mm) and the default sku only; copy once per model
cp config/watches/breitling-superocean42-a17375211b1s1-measure.json config/watches/breitling-<model>-$sku-measure.json   # MEASURE every value again on the new photo (list below)
python3 -B -m pipeline.breitling_<model>_prep $sku         # 2x copy, trace, dial_photo.jpg, steel_photo.jpg
cp config/watches/breitling-superocean42-a17375211b1s1.json config/watches/breitling-<model>-$sku.json   # change model, trace, dial_map, steel_map, verified_mm, case tips, ring_centre_mm, crown, hands, strap, emissions
cp jobs/breitling-superocean42-a17375211b1s1.json jobs/breitling-<model>-$sku.json                  # replace the id / output / spec / trace strings; step stays watch_breitling_superocean42
cp config/presets/watch_kit_white_front_breitling_superocean42.json config/presets/watch_kit_white_front_breitling_<model>.json   # scale = 2 x px_per_mm of the trace, nothing else
python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs validate jobs/breitling-<model>-$sku.json && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/breitling-<model>-$sku.json --force
cp outputs/jobs/breitling-<model>-$sku/breitling-<model>-$sku.glb models/watches/breitling-$sku.glb
```

**Template (keep as is, per the Black Bay 54 / Pelagos recipe):** the step `pipeline/steps/watch_breitling_superocean42.py` (case from the silhouette, bezel ring lathe, crystal, dial lathe with the photo map, hands from profile / lume lists, crown, strap `kind` "rubber" or "bracelet"), the kit lighting and camera (only the scale changes), materials (`steel_gap`, `lume`, `crystal`), pose and depth scale, heights (`case`, `bezel`, `crystal`, `dial`, `hands.z`, `crown.z`), steel emission 0.31, strap emission 0.30, hands emission 0.43, dial emission 0.30 (black dial; check a light dial both ways), all `*_photo` material logic, the two comparison scripts' conventions (below).

**Per watch (re-measure on the photo, never copy):**
- Photo conventions: Breitling = 1024 x 1024 WebP with alpha, upright render with slight camera tilt. Positions are in continuous px (pixel i spans [i, i+1]); the 2x working copy puts the pivot at (2p, 2p); the comparison frame moves the 2x pivot to (1024, 1024).
- Scale: fit a circle to the left silhouette (rows around the pivot row, +-100 px) = bezel outer diameter = Catalog size; `bezel_out_circle_px` = [cx, cy, r]. Chronographs and tool watches with a flat-ring bezel use the same fit; a round case without a bezel would use the case side.
- Pivot: intersect the centre lines of the hour and minute hands (silhouette centres in rotated strips); the hub pin hole can be 1 to 2 px lower (hands sit higher than the dial under the camera tilt). Check that the ring circle centres (outer silhouette, ceramic inner edge, coin ring gap) are within 1 px and set `ring_centre_mm`.
- Rings (px about the ring centre): radial scans (mean grey over 10 degree sectors, r in 0.5 px steps) on the left side show the dark lines of the insert's inner and outer edges, the bright coin ring and the rehaul lip: `bezel_in`, `insert_out`, `bezel_out`, `crystal_edge`, `dial_flat`.
- **Bezel (Breitling specific):** ceramic or aluminium insert with a gradient (Superocean: black to grey, numerals only in the print), inside a steel coin-edge ring: one lathe, appearance from the projected photo; a bezel with a tachymeter / 60-minute scale (Chronomat, Navitimer) is also only print, nothing to model. (Untested.) A Navitimer slide-rule bezel is a flat ring in the photo: use the same lathe with `insert_out` = ring end (no coin edge: set `insert_out` = `bezel_out`, a coin ring of zero width).
- **Crown with guards:** box = crown (x from the flank to the cap end, y extent), `crown.teeth`, `fluted_to_mm` (where fluting ends), `cap_radius_fraction`; the guards are NOT modelled: they stay in the alpha silhouette (the prep replaces only the crown rows by the mirrored left edge, with a 0.05 mm margin because the guards touch the crown). Chronograph pushers (Chronomat, Navitimer) are outside this template: they are separate cylinders on the 2 and 4 o'clock axis; add a part in a copy of the step (a prism along the angle from the pivot, like the crown) and keep the guards/pushers in the silhouette rows.
- **Hands:** angle (axis through the pivot) and lengths from silhouettes in rotated strips (threshold about 60 grey on a black dial; use the light side threshold on a light dial); profile = [mm along the hand, half width] tail to tip, the last point at the measured length; lume = polygon over the bright core. Breitling hands are blocky: bar lume (hour), block lume on the minute hand, thin red-tipped / lollipop seconds; chronograph subdials add small hands: leave them to the photographed dial map (only the three main hands are 3D). A hand tip hidden under another (minute over seconds) is measured on the visible part only.
- **Strap / bracelet:** rubber: `strap.kind` "rubber", `centre_x_mm` = [|y| mm, left x, right x] seam tables per side (std-of-brightness scan every 10 px along the seams, textured sides vs smooth centre), `pieces_mm` per section (side from the lug tops, centre from under the bezel, both to the alpha ends), `edge_from` (a row just beyond the lug tops where the silhouette is strap-wide), `case.tip_top` / `tip_bottom` (lug top / bottom rows, on the 0.25 mm grid so that their sum is a multiple of 0.25). A steel bracelet (Chronomat Rouleaux, Navitimer bracelet) = `strap.kind` "bracelet" is also in the step but only with the three-column layout of the Black Bay 54; for other layouts copy the Pelagos 39 step's `columns` / `bounds_x_mm` / `pieces_mm` generalisation into a copy of this step.
- **Logo / print handling:** logos, numerals, subdials, date windows and the cream or coloured chapter ring are part of the 2x dial photo map (the photographed hands stay in it, hidden by the 3D hands); a date window is not cut out. If the catalogue render carries a badge or a second image beside the watch the prep keeps only the largest alpha component (already in this prep).
- Emissions: check region ratios with the same region list as above (dial dark / light, bezel, coin ring, strap sections, lugs, crown, whole watch); the response is nonlinear and the dark dial floor does not follow the emission.
