## Zoom check

`zoom_check.jpg` (3x crops of the 1280 frames, reference | render): hands and hub, centre of the dial, lower dial / 6 o'clock. Sharp: the applied baton indices and the double bars at 3, 9 and 6 (shape, bevel, shadow edge), the minute track bars and the black square markers, the SWISS MADE line with its coronet, the SUPERLATIVE CHRONOMETER / OFFICIALLY CERTIFIED text, the hub disc and screw, the hour, minute and seconds hands (edges, black facets and lume strip line up with the photo within about 1 to 2 px; flat tips and the counterweight tail match), the seconds needle crossing "SUPERLATIVE" and the 6 index. Soft: the dial print (ROLEX, OYSTER PERPETUAL) renders visibly softer than the INTER_AREA reference in these crops (film filter plus the half-scale comparison frame; the shipped textures are native 22.8 px/mm, so the real zoom in the app is 2x sharper than these crops); the thin black bezel rim lines and the bracelet grain are slightly softer; narrow black hand facets read a little grey. Fixed in the pass: the outer bottom bracelet pieces used to start at the lug tip and showed a bright bevel line (clipped light band); they now start 1.4 mm inside the case (hidden by the slab) and the band is gone. No halos beyond the 1 to 2 px hand margin described above, no dark gaps, stair-steps or misplaced hand edges were visible in the crops.
