What differs from the 126334 template (everything else is the template):
- Photo: dial = slate sunburst with GREEN applied Roman numerals (Wimbledon style: XII I II X III IIII VI V, a white lume bar at 9, no baton indices); the tracker row says "slate / Jubilee", which fits (the green numerals are only a dial detail). Hands, coronet, bezel and bracelet centre are yellow gold; the case flank and outer bracelet links are steel (two-tone comes entirely from the one projected steel photo, no separate material; the gold needs no separate emission: gold regions landed within 1-2 %).
- Hands: the template prep's colour test (r / g against a green dial) does not work here. Variant prep `pipeline/rolex_datejust41_126333_prep.py` adds `hand_score: "gold"` to the measure file: mask = chroma r - b >= `hand_chroma` (14; the slate dial and shadows have r - b about 0, the green numerals are negative) OR white lume (grey >= 215), per hand `chroma_only` for the seconds needle (white print "J" of DATEJUST touches its counterweight tail). The ROI polygons are the 126334's (same hands). Traced: hour 8.675 / tail 2.688 mm, minute 13.397 / 2.663, seconds 14.294 / 4.681; overlaid at 4x: outlines follow the gold edges within about 1 px.
- Head: pivot, rings, flutes (74, long parity 1), crown, Cyclops outline and tip rows are the 126334's measure values; the overlay check found zero offset (render vs photo mean abs difference is minimal at shift 0 px on all bracelet bands and the head, silhouette IoU 0.9948).
- Jubilee bracelet (spec `strap.bracelet`): columns outer / semi / centre / semi / outer; column lines x 860 / 926.6 / 993.1 / 1059.6 px top (860 / 926.6 / 993.3 / 1059.6 bottom): the three gold columns are equal (about 3 mm each, 66.6 px), the outer columns are brushed steel to the silhouette. Outer joints (px): top 904 (lug) / 807 / 730 / 685 / 674 end; bottom 1980 (lug) / 2107 / 2227 / 2331.5 / 2422 / 2490 / 2526 end (gradient maxima on x 790..850 and 1070..1130, left and right identical). Gold semi and centre columns: joints at the centres of the dark gap runs (luminance < 70 on a +-14 px band) next to each polished block, read separately for top and bottom (semi top 862.5 / 833.5 / 772.5 / 759.5 / 711 / 680 / 674; semi bottom 1997.5 ... 2494.5 / 2530; centre top 876 / 813 / 795 / 737 / 728 / 694 / 678.5 / 673.5; centre bottom 2002.5 ... 2511.5 / 2530; full lists in the spec). The semi and centre columns are offset by half a pitch as in the real Jubilee. z: outer 3.4, semi and centre 3.5.
- Emissions (spec): dial 0.30, Cyclops 0.30, steel (case, bezel, crown, bracelet) 0.29, hands and hub 0.36, specular 0 everywhere, crystal alpha 0.02; nothing is baked beyond the photo's own shading.

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1280 frame, render : photo): whole watch 1.005, dial plate 0.997 (109.6 vs 110.0), dial centre 1.000, dial outer ring 0.993, dial dark areas 1.044, dial bright features 0.971, rehaut ring 0.973, flange 1.015, chamfer 1.030, flutes 1.016, case flank band 0.979, Cyclops 1.006, bracelet top outer link 1.011, top gold columns 1.017, bracelet bottom outer link 1.002, bottom gold columns 1.009, lug top 1.023, lug bottom 0.992, crown 1.007. Known differences: the background and alpha rim are not part of the asset; the dial print, Roman numerals with their green frames, lume bar, coronet, date numeral, rehaut engraving, the hands' lume strips, black facets and polished edges, the flutes' bright / dark shading, the bracelet's brushed grain, polished bevels and the dark gap shadows between the gold links are texture only (photo projection); the geometry is the 3D hands (flat traced outlines), Cyclops (flat top, no real magnification), 74 flutes (flat facets, depth assumed), crown (18 coarse flutes), crystal, dial plane, bracelet links (flat-topped slabs with 0.06 mm gaps over a dark backing) and the case slab; the dark gap runs of the gold links read lighter in the render than in the photo (photo grey 12 vs render 16-23 on the darkest pixels: the emission lifts darks, the usual residue; the means are within 2 %); the small strips between the polished blocks of the gold columns are modelled as short flat pieces, not as the stepped overlapping plates; the steel outer links' rounded corners come from the silhouette only; heights have no side view, back flat.
