# Baseline: AUDEMARS PIGUET Royal Oak Selfwinding 41 15500ST grey (saved 2026-11-07, awaiting designer approval)

Front-relief build on the 41 mm steel / grey Grande Tapisserie dial / integrated steel bracelet catalogue render (1200 x 1626 rendition), **follower of the Royal Oak 41 template** (shared step and prep; own kit preset with this photo's scale). The white measure values were carried onto this photo by an ECC affine fit and the first render already registered within 0.26 px (2x frame) on screws, hub, flats, crown and bracelet patches; only emissions were tuned afterwards (no geometry correction passes). The catalog gives the very same photo (`standup-1014`) to row 90 (15510ST, "Steel, grey"), so this GLB is also published under that name by the lead. Approval is the designer's; this file is not a similarity score.

Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.audemars_royaloak41_prep 15500st-grey && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/audemars-royaloak41-15500st-grey.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/audemars-royaloak41-15500st-grey/audemars-royaloak41-15500st-grey.glb models/watches/audemars-15500st-grey.glb`.

State: as the template (steel parts, hands and hub carry the photo projected from the front; dial = 2x photo map with the photographed hands; crystal alpha 0.02; one hexagonal crown; integrated bracelet from the measured seams and bars), with: dial emission 0.33 (white 0.34, black 0.30), steel emission 0.28 (white 0.30, black 0.31), hands emission 0.45. Scale: left flat anchored at 20.5 mm = 6.9761 px/mm by the prep (the affine fit puts this photo at white x 1.0009 / 1.0012, i.e. the anchor reads about 0.3 % high; all pixel values convert with the same S, so the comparison frame is consistent), pivot (600.22, 792.13).

Registration (phase correlation of photo vs render patches, 2x px): 8 screws within 0.15, hub 0.05, top / bottom flats 0.14 / 0.34, crown 0.14, six bracelet patches 0.01..0.12; the lone left-flat patch reads 0.79 / 0.46 (low correlation response, edge only) and the right-flat patch is uninformative. Silhouette edges within 1 px at 2x on the case and bracelet rows (the near-white bracelet top end cannot be thresholded against the white render background; the crown tip corners are 6..11 px square in the render, see below). Hands: brightness profiles across the hour / minute / seconds hands put the centre lines within 0.1..0.4 px of the photo's (2x), the hour tip within 0.2 px; the minute and seconds tips are contaminated by the baton indices in the profile and were judged in the 2x zoom (coincide). Hand angles 303.8 / 49.9 / 227.3 deg, lengths 8.95 / 13.70 / 14.44 mm (template values; hand lengths in the trace use S = 6.9761).

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1280 frame, render : photo): whole watch 1.011, dial plate (r < 200 px) 1.006, dial bright features (photo > 150) 0.985, dial dark areas (photo <= 150) 1.023, bezel face 1.006, bracelet top (photo rows 588..620) 1.020, bracelet bottom (985..1060) 0.986, case top (632..648) 1.025, case bottom (946..972) 0.974, crown 1.002. Mean abs grey difference over the silhouette 6.40. Emission trials (dial / steel -> plate ratio / mean abs difference): 0.34 / 0.30 -> 1.010 / 6.79; 0.32 / 0.30 -> 1.002 / 6.85; 0.32 / 0.29 -> 1.002 / 6.61; 0.30 / 0.29 -> 0.993 / 6.72; 0.32 / 0.28 -> 1.002 / 6.44; 0.33 / 0.28 -> 1.006 / 6.40 (kept: lowest mean abs difference; plate and bright / dark features straddle 1 on both sides). Steel response is weak (0.01 = 0.4 %), case top and bottom stay split by slope shading (one value for all steel).

Known differences: as the black and white baselines (background / alpha rim not part of the asset; Grande Tapisserie waffle, baton indices, print, date window, minute track, facets and inlay of the hands, screw slots, brushed / polished finishes are texture only; bracelet seams are open gaps over a dark backing, so the render's seams read slightly heavier; rounded pin-link ends are flat texture on continuous bars; bracelet ends about 2 px short and square; crown is a plain hexagon, its tip corners extend 6..11 px (2x) beyond the photo's chamfered ones and it has no base gap line; 7 px/mm source, the 2x copy adds no detail; heights have no side view, back flat; silhouette includes about 2 px of catalogue-render tilt parallax). Grey-dial specific: the 3D hands are a touch brighter than the photographed ones beside the indices, dark waffle areas 2 % light, the polished bezel flats and the chamfer shading are texture only.
