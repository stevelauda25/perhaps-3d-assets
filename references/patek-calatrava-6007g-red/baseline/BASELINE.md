Follower of the Calatrava 6007G template ([blue baseline](../../patek-calatrava-6007g-blue/baseline/BASELINE.md)): step, prep and kit preset reused read-only, no code change. Approval is the designer's; this file is not a similarity score.

Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.patek_calatrava6007g_prep 6007g-red && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/patek-calatrava6007g-6007g-red.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/patek-calatrava6007g-6007g-red/patek-calatrava6007g-6007g-red.glb models/watches/patek-6007g-red.glb`.

Registration against the blue photo (same studio camera): phase correlation of the dial region 0.003 / 0.005 px; the hour and minute hands are pixel-identical; the red seconds hand lies on the blue hand's pixels (hub x 931..988 at row 1258, axis 179.3..179.5 deg); silhouette xor 5,389 px of 2.0 million (strap end rows 309..351 / 2521..2557 where the dark alpha edge differs, a few pocket rows 671..676 / 1911..1918). Hence circle (956.50, 1271.23) r 626.35 px, pivot (960.0, 1258.3), rings, hands, crown, lug rows and pocket rows are the blue values; the prep recomputes the silhouette rows, pockets and strap end shape from the red alpha. Per photo only the colours differ: red markers and minute-track ticks, red seconds hand, red stitching, red lining, and a different leather emboss (woven checker, texture only).

State: as the blue build (steel emission 0.275, hands 0.34, dial 0.31, crystal alpha 0.02) except the strap emission 0.23 (blue 0.20): strap top 1.009 and bottom 0.975 at 0.20, top 1.022 and bottom 0.990 at 0.23; the red photo's strap bottom is darker than the blue one's, so one value cannot centre both halves (top reads brighter than bottom at equal emission, as in the blue build). The dial emission stayed 0.31: the red accents are small areas on a black dial (red marks 0.968, red seconds hand 0.995 / 1.004, red lining 0.965, red stitching 0.985).

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 2048 frame, render : photo): whole watch 1.001 (mean abs grey difference over the silhouette 8.2, mostly edges and emboss), dial all 1.005, dial dark ring (photo grey < 60, r > 330 px) 1.128 (26.5 vs 23.5), dial guilloche centre 1.004, dial bright features (grey > 120) 0.942, dial red marks 0.968, bezel 1.006, case ring 0.981, strap top 1.022 (upper 1.025, lower 1.021), strap bottom 0.990, red lining 0.965, red stitching 0.985, lug top 1.011, lug bottom 0.991, crown 0.960, hour hand 1.009, minute hand 1.006, red seconds pointer 0.995, tail 1.004.

Known differences: the background, the faint drop shadow below the strap and the soft alpha rim are not part of the asset; the black dial ring reads about 3 grey levels lighter than the photo (black floor follows the emission), the bright print about 6 percent dark (render softness at 0.75 x); the guilloche, numerals, date window, minute track, red markers and logo are texture only (flat); hour / minute batons are plain tapered slabs (lume and shading are in the projected photo); the red hand is a flat polygon; crown flutes are 20 uniform straight flutes with a smooth cone cap (crown 4 percent dark); the leather emboss and the red stitching are the flat photo projection (no 3D emboss, no stitching relief), strap top corners chamfered rather than rounded; red lining between the lugs is the slab's flat photo (3.5 percent dark, lug inner flanks not modelled); bezel / lug heights are the blue build's guesses (no side view), back flat. Only grey luminance was compared; the hue of the red parts was judged by eye on the side-by-side, not measured. Not verified: designer approval.
