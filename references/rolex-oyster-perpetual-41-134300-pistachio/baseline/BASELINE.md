State: as the green build. Verification of the template geometry: the alpha mask is identical to the green photo (0 differing pixels), and the pistachio comparison render differs from the green one by at most 1 grey level outside the dial disc (mean 0.008, no pixel above 20), so registration, case outline (silhouette IoU 0.994 as measured for green), crown and bracelet joints hold.
