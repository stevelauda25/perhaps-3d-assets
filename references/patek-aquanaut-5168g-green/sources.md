Catalog 2 line (row 102): Patek Philippe, Aquanaut, 5168G, 42.2mm, White gold, green, Rubber strap, Pre-owned, 105000 / 110250. The strap colour field is not stated for this row (row 104, the blue 5168G, says "White gold, blue"); the photo shows a khaki green strap matching the green dial, which is what the asset follows.
