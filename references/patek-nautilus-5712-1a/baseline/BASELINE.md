# Baseline: PATEK PHILIPPE Nautilus 5712/1A (saved 2026-11-07, awaiting designer approval)

Front-relief build on the 40 mm steel / blue embossed dial with moon phase, power reserve and date / steel bracelet catalogue photo, which is only a 350 x 480 px JPEG (white background, no alpha; the host serves no larger version). FOLLOWER of the Nautilus template (5811/1G template step and kit lighting, built on for the 5711/1A); own prep copy, step copy, measure, spec, job and kit scale. One build, one small shape pass (end-piece corner radii); no emission pass was needed (region ratios were within 1 to 2 % on the first build, so the 5711/1A emissions held). Approval is the designer's; this file is not a similarity score.

**Honest note on the source.** The photo is about 6.6 px/mm (the 5811/1G photo is 33.6 px/mm, five times more). Geometry, joints and the silhouette were measured on a x4 Lanczos enlargement to about +-1 source px (+-0.15 mm at 40 mm); the dial print, hands, indices, minute dots, sub-dial numerals and needles, moon disc and brushed grain are reproduced only at the photo's own resolution (enlarging does not recover detail; no lettering, grain or geometry was invented), so this GLB is soft at zoom compared with the 5811/1G.

Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.patek_nautilus40_5712_prep 57121a && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/patek-nautilus40-57121a.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/patek-nautilus40-57121a/patek-nautilus40-57121a.glb models/watches/patek-57121a.glb`.

Source handling (recorded): the original stays untouched. The prep enlarges it x4 (Lanczos, 1400 x 1920, 26.3123 working px/mm) and builds the silhouette by a flood fill of the near-white background from the border (fixed range, tolerance 12, 3x3 opening, 0.8 px Gaussian soft edge). Two leaks, both cut in the measure file: (1) as on the 5711/1A the soft drop shadow below the bracelet reads as foreground; `mask.clip` removes it with hand-measured bracelet edge lines (left x 451 / 466 / 480 / 494 / 500, right 959 / 942 / 934 / 925 / 918 at rows 1690 / 1740 / 1780 / 1810 / 1818, then a rounded end bulge x about 604..812 down to row 1836 where the clasp-side end piece sticks out); (2) NEW on this photo, the polished white outer band of the bezel is white on the white background at the upper left (rows 570..650, up to 30 working px = 1.1 mm), where the flood fill leaked into the band and would have cut the bezel back; `mask.union_bezel_outer` 1.0 adds the measured bezel superellipse to the silhouette (the band is checked against the visible band edge on the right, left and bottom, where it is not white on white). Checked by contour overlay on the case, ears, crown, bracelet sides and ends. The scale is anchored on both flanks (left x 177 over rows 840..1000, right x 1229 over rows 760..830 and 985..1040, 40 mm -> 26.3123 px/mm, 0.7 % below the 5711/1A's 26.506: the ear flanks differ slightly between the two photos).

Pivot: the hour and minute lume centre lines were fitted on strips rotated to each hand (hour compass 302.2 deg over s 110..185, minute 53.5 deg over s 70..290) and intersect at (700.4, 887.3); the hub disc centre is (702, 889.5), 2.7 px (0.1 mm) away (camera tilt; the hub stays in the dial map, the 3D hub disc carries the projected photo). The pivot used is the line intersection, (700.5, 887.5), so the 3D hands sit on the photographed hands.

State: steel parts (case, bezel, crown, bracelet backing) carry the photo projected from the front (steel_photo material, specular 0, roughness 0.6, emission 0.285); bracelet rows and polished centre pieces use the same photo with their own emissions (top 0.275, bottom 0.29); hands / hub / lume `hands_photo` emission 0.43 (the photographed hands stay in the dial map, the 3D hands sit over them at z 7.4 / 7.65, the hub cap at 7.9 + 0.1); dial plate = photo map with emission 0.34, specular 0; crystal alpha 0.02 (alpha-blended); the window / dial core is a dark non-photo `steel_gap` / `lume` set as in the 5811. Case outline = silhouette (ears included) between the end-link tips (+20.5 / -22.0 mm), crown rows 833..976 replaced by the right flank line. Measured (working px at 26.3123 px/mm): bezel outer superellipse centre (702, 890), half axes 455 x 469, exponent 2.35, 32 px polished band, window scanned 12.7 to 14.2 mm from the pivot, hands hour 302.2 deg (7.88 mm, half width 16 px) / minute 53.5 deg (11.78 mm, half width 13 px), hub discs 30 / 14 px, crown axis row 906, base x 1188, max radius 66 px (2.5 mm), tip x 1263, top joints (centres of the dark bands) rows 184.5 / 266.5 / 354, bottom joints 1459.5 / 1570 / 1663 / 1734.5 (end 1818, bulge to 1834), 5 top and 6 bottom polished pieces. The 5711/1A hand outlines (same Patek parts) were rescaled per hand: hour widths x0.946 and lengths x1.006, minute widths x0.89 and lengths x1.014.

**What differs from the 5711/1A (the template for this build):** only hour and minute are 3D hands (there is no central seconds hand); the power-reserve needle, the moon-phase / retrograde-date pointer on its own pivot, the small-seconds needle, the date marker and all sub-dial print stay in the dial map as photographed (their needles are 0.2 mm hair lines at 6.6 px/mm: not modelled in 3D); the bracelet end is a rounded bulge (clasp-side end piece) and the top end a dome, modelled with larger corner radii on the end pieces (top end 17 px, bottom end 16 px) and the rounded mask bulge; joints are the centres of 20 to 40 px thick dark bands here (the 5711/1A had thin joint lines), so each gap in the 3D rows is only the 0.05 mm plus 6 px row clearance and the dark band is carried by the projected photo; pivot = hand-line intersection rather than the hub centre; crown larger (66 px against 60).

Emissive parts and strengths (all in the spec): steel_photo 0.285, bracelet top 0.275, bracelet bottom 0.29, hands_photo 0.43, dial 0.34; no specular on photo parts (specular 0, roughness 0.6), no baked shadows beyond the photo's own; the photo's studio lighting is baked into the maps, so an app environment adds little on top.

Region mean luminance (sRGB grey, 1280 frame, render : photo): dial plate 1.002 (91.8 vs 91.6 grey levels), dial bright features 1.003, dial dark areas 1.002, bezel face 1.004, polished band 1.003, bracelet top 0.999, bracelet bottom 0.989, case top lug 1.013, case bottom lug 0.978 (the template's end-link ramp shading, not tuned per side), left ear 1.005, right ear 1.007, crown 0.939 (see below). Mean abs grey difference over the photo silhouette 3.3. Hand registration (bright masks along each hand line): IoU hour 0.981, minute 0.975. Silhouette IoU 0.959 (render not near-white vs the flood-fill mask; the difference is the white polished band that is white on white, the soft edge and the end bulge, not a geometric miss).

Known differences: the background and the soft drop shadow below the bracelet are not part of the asset; the embossed lines, applied indices, print, sub-dials, moon disc, minute dots, date marker, brushed / polished facets, crown flutes and the bracelet's grain are texture only and as soft as the 350 px photo; the 3D hour / minute hands, hub, crown, bezel band / chamfer step, dial plane, bracelet rows and polished pieces are geometry; the upper-left white band edge is indeterminate in the photo (white on white), so the bezel outer edge there follows the superellipse measured elsewhere and the render shows a faint bevel line a few frame px outside the photo's visible grey band; the crown is a lathe with a mean radius profile (the photo's crown rim is nearly white at its right edge and the lathe tip is rounder: crown region 6 % dark); the top end dome and bottom end bulge are rounded rectangles (their arcs are texture); the case slab end links slope toward the bracelet by a straight ramp; heights have no side view, back flat. Textures: the steel map is 3x the source (854 x 1400) and the dial map 2.7x (698 px): more would only store the Lanczos blur.

## Zoom check

`zoom_check.jpg` (3x crops, reference | render): upper dial, hands and hub, lower dial. What matches: the PATEK PHILIPPE print, the applied indices with their lume inlays, the double baton at 12, the power-reserve scale and needle, the red date marker, the moon disc and stars, the date ring numerals, the small-seconds sub-dial, the hour / minute hands (edges, lume line and round tips line up with the photo within about 1 px of the half-size frame), the hub and the bezel edge. What is soft and why: everything fine (GENEVE, the sub-dial numerals and ticks, the embossing gradient, the bezel's brushed grain) is exactly as soft as the 350 px photo at a 3x zoom (equal to 12x of the source pixels: lettering edges are blurred, JPEG block noise is visible in the print and numerals); the brushed grain on the bezel face is a little smoother in the render than in the photo. The sub-dial needles are flat (texture) because they are 0.2 mm hair lines in the source. No halos, dark gaps, stair-steps or misplaced hand edges were visible in the crops.
