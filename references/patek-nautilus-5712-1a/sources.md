Working copy: no file is written; `pipeline/patek_nautilus40_5712_prep.py` enlarges the original x4 with Lanczos in memory (working image 1400 x 1920, 26.3 working px per mm = 6.6 source px per mm) and builds the silhouette by a flood fill of the near-white background from the border (tolerance 12, fixed range, 3x3 opening, 0.8 px Gaussian soft edge). Two leaks are cut in `config/watches/patek-nautilus40-57121a-measure.json`: the flood fill reads the drop shadow below the bracelet as foreground (`mask.clip`: bracelet edge lines left / right from working row 1690, a rounded end bulge, bottom row 1836), and it leaks into the bezel's white polished outer band at the upper left (white on white, rows 570..650, up to 30 working px), so `mask.union_bezel_outer` adds the measured bezel superellipse to the silhouette. Enlargement does not recover detail: geometry, joints and the silhouette can be measured to about +-1 source px (+-0.15 mm), the dial print, hands, indices, sub-dials and brushed grain are only as sharp as the 350 px photo.

Maps written by the prep to `outputs/watches/patek-nautilus40-57121a/`: `dial_photo.jpg` (698 px crop of the x4 working image, equal to 2.7 map px per source px: no extra detail, smooth) and `steel_photo.jpg` (the whole watch, capped at 1400 px on the long side = 3x the source).

Catalog 2 line (row 109): Patek Philippe, Nautilus, 5712/1A, 40mm, Steel, blue, Bracelet, Pre-owned, 160000 / 168000. The photo shows a blue gradient horizontally embossed dial with a power-reserve sub-dial at 9 (needle to 48 / 36), a moon-phase aperture with a retrograde date arc at 7 (central pointer on its own pivot), a small seconds sub-dial at 4:30 and a date-marker aperture at 6; the asset follows the photo.
Patek Philippe image rights remain with the manufacturer; reference use only.
