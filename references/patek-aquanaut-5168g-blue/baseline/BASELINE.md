## Registration against the green (why a prep copy and own measures)

The blue photo is the same studio render family but a different pose; the green template was NOT valid in pixels: scale 29.719 vs 29.850 px/mm (0.4 % smaller), hub (959.56, 1334.86) vs (959.0, 1354.5), and parts at different heights shift against each other (lug tips 24.25 / 26.5 mm from the hub instead of 24.75 / 25.75, bezel centre 3.4 px below the hub, crown axis 15 px below the hub instead of 2, strap ends 31.5 / 40.75 mm, strap walls taper instead of flare). The silhouette overlay after hub / scale alignment still differs by up to about 20 px at the lug and strap ends, so every pixel value was re-measured on this photo. Hand centre lines sit 3.7 px (hour, left) and 2.6 px (minute, right) off the hub centre (camera tilt); the green prep could not model that, so `pipeline/patek_aquanaut42_blue_prep.py` (copy of the green prep, untouched original) writes `hands.<name>.offset_mm` into the trace, which the template step already supports, and downsizes the steel map to 2048 px.

## State

As the 5168G green: steel parts carry the photo projected from the front, steel emission 0.282; non-metallic `strap_photo` rubber, emission 0.22 top / 0.308 bottom; hands / hub / lume `hands_photo` 0.43; native dial photo map emission 0.323; crystal alpha 0.02; specular 0 and roughness 0.6 (steel) / 0.8 (rubber) / 0.35 (dial) so an app environment light adds little on top; no extra baked shadows. Case outline = alpha silhouette with the crown rows 1267..1434 replaced by the line at 42.2 mm from the left flank. Blue specific: scale 29.7191 px/mm anchored on the left flank (332.49 px = 21.1 mm from the pivot); bezel outer superellipse centre (0, -0.114) mm, half axes 21.1 x 20.887 mm, exponent 2.25; window scanned 15.81 to 16.98 mm from the pivot; case tips y +24.25 / -26.5 mm; strap pieces 18.8 to 31.4 (top) and 40.65 (bottom) mm; hands hour 303.58 deg, minute 51.08 deg, seconds 179.51 deg with centre-line offsets -0.124 / +0.088 / -0.017 mm (hand shapes, lengths and the 5.13 mm seconds tail are the green's mm profiles, edge widths checked on the blue photo); hub discs 39.8 / 21 px; crown radius 2.81 mm (83.4 px, so the crown fills the replaced rows exactly), axial 3.89 mm, axis y -0.526 mm.

## Zoom check

`zoom_check.jpg` (3x crops: logo and 12 o'clock print, hands and hub, 6 o'clock and small print) plus extra crops of the hands, crown and strap / bezel junctions (viewed, not stored).
- Sharp and matching: PATEK PHILIPPE / GENEVE print, applied numerals and indices, minute track, SWISS MADE, the embossed grid lines and the blue gradient are the photo's own pixels (native, not enlarged), so they read as sharp as the 29.7 px/mm source; hour / minute hand outlines and lume inlays, the seconds hand and the white hub sit on the photographed ones with no visible doubled edge at 3x.
- Soft: the photographed bezel brushing, the crown flutes and the strap's grain are flat texture (no relief) and a little softer than the source because the half-scale comparison frame is 2x smaller than the photo; the hour-hand base disc shading under the hub is geometry plus photo, slightly flatter than the photo.
- Residuals: a one-pixel pale hairline under the crown at the case shoulder (the 0.25 mm silhouette grid meets the crown's lower edge); the faint grey haze to the right of the crown is the render's soft light card, not the photo; lug top reads 2 % light and lug bottom 1.7 % dark (shading difference between the photo's facets and the projection); the hands are flat-shaded 3D over the photographed ones, so their facets show the photo's lighting only.

## Known differences

The background, the soft drop shadow and the alpha rim are not part of the asset; the embossed grid, applied numerals, indices, minute-track flange, date window, brushed / polished facets, crown flutes and the strap's embossed pad are texture only; the 3D hands, crown, bezel / chamfer step and dial plane are geometry; lug inner walls and polished lug facets are flat photo texture; the strap / lug junction is a flat overlay (strap inner walls are straight lines between measured points, within about 5 px native near the lower lug); hand pivot offsets by height beyond the per-hand centre-line offset are not modelled; heights have no side view (real thickness about 8.1 mm), back flat; hand tips and tail were not re-measured independently (numerals and the 6 o'clock index touch them) and follow the green's mm profile, consistent with the blue photo at the visible edges.

## Template note (not needed for other variants)

The blue-specific prep copy is the one to use for any further 5168G photo whose hand centre lines are off the hub or whose steel map is above 2048 px; otherwise `pipeline/patek_aquanaut42_prep.py` is enough.
