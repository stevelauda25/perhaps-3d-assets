Known differences: the background and alpha rim are not part of the asset; the dial print (incl. DEEPSEA in green, the 12800ft = 3900m line, the date numeral and window), rehaut engraving, applied indices, minute track, the bezel numerals / scale / lume pearl, the large white reflection on the ceramic (baked in the photo, so the "black" bezel is half white exactly as photographed), the lume strips and black facets of the hands, the coin edge's flute shading, the crown flutes' shading and the bracelet's brushed grain and polished bevels are texture only (photo projection); the 3D hands (flat traced outlines), crown (16 coarse flutes, square upper corners where the photo's crown has chamfered ones), ceramic insert, rim and coin ring (60 flutes of 0.065 mm), crystal, dial plane, bracelet links and case slab are geometry.
