Rebuild: `python3 -B -m pipeline.audemars_royaloakchrono41_prep 26240st-green && python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/audemars-royaloakchrono41-26240st-green.json --force`, then `cp outputs/jobs/audemars-royaloakchrono41-26240st-green/audemars-royaloakchrono41-26240st-green.glb models/watches/audemars-26240st-green.glb`.

State: same recipe as the white variant (steel parts, bracelet links and backing carry the projected photo, emission 0.31, specular 0; hands and hub `hands_photo`, emission 0.43; dial = 2x photo map with the photographed hands, sub-dials, waffle and print in it, specular 0; crystal alpha 0.02; case outline = the alpha silhouette with the three right-side crown / pusher rows replaced by straight lines). Values that are the white template's because the green photo registers to it (checked: silhouette within 0.2 px, octagon flats within 0.15 px, bracelet joint rows within 0.3 px, crown / pushers identical): scale 6.9268 px/mm, octagon, crown_replace lines, crowns / pushers, bracelet pieces and backing, lug tips (top 24.0, bottom 26.75 mm), hand profiles (same shape and angles, hour 304.0 deg, minute 50.3 deg, chrono-seconds tail 227.3 deg; the 3D hands coincide with the photographed ones on a 2.2x overlay). Values re-measured on the green photo: pivot (599.2, 793.1) = white pivot + (-0.2, +0.2) (screw-head centroids average (-0.22, +0.21) from the white ones, hub symmetry search gives the same delta), screws = white + that delta, **opening circle r 112.38 about (599.53, 792.30)** (rms 0.22 px over 348 of 360 scans; the same code on the white photo gives r 110.46, so the opening is 1.9 px = 0.27 mm larger here: opening centre in mm (0.048, 0.115), radius 16.224), octagon right 741.4 / bottom 936.15 px. **Dial emission 0.31** (white 0.35): trials 0.335 -> plate 1.017, 0.29 -> 0.997, 0.31 -> 1.006 (bright print / indices drop to 0.95 at the low end, the plate dominates the choice).

Known differences: the same as the white baseline (background not part of the asset; waffle, three sub-dials and their hands, date window, printed logo, chapter-ring slope, polished chamfer bands, the crown cap end and pusher cap bands and the polished pins across the bracelet joints are texture; chrono-seconds needle above the pivot hidden behind the minute hand and not modelled; bracelet / case ends cut about 2 px inside the photo's ends; lug tops read about 6 % light (one steel emission value); pushers read light (nut hex faces); catalogue render with slight tilt; no side view, back flat). Green-specific: the bright print / applied indices read about 5 % dark (dial emission compromise), the dark green dial areas follow the photo within a few percent; the 0.2-0.3 px offsets between the two photos are below what the 2x map can show.

Template note: the white BASELINE.md "Template for the next Royal Oak Chronograph variants" applies as is; for another dial colour of this model, copy the green measure / spec / job, re-measure per photo (the opening circle and the pivot shift came out different here) and re-tune only the dial emission.
