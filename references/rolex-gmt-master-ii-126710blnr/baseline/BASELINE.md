## Template for the next GMT-Master II variants (126720VTNR left crown Oyster, 126711CHNR Everose Oyster, 126713GRNR Jubilee two-tone, 126710BLRO Oyster)

The step, prep and kit preset are generic (spec / measure / trace driven; variant-named for the GMT-Master II 40 family and used READ-ONLY by key). Photos are in `references/rolex-gmt-master-ii-<sku>/rolex-<sku>_original.png` (check with `ls references | grep gmt-master`). A variant needs only its own measure / spec / job JSON.

```sh
k=126713grnr; ref=rolex-gmt-master-ii-$k; photo=$(ls references/$ref/*_original.png)    # k = 126720vtnr | 126711chnr | 126713grnr | 126710blro (lowercase)
python3 - "$k" "$photo" <<'PY'
import json, sys
k, photo = sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2]
m = json.load(open("config/watches/rolex-gmtmaster40-126710blnr-measure.json")); m["photo"] = photo
json.dump(m, open("config/watches/rolex-gmtmaster40-%s-measure.json" % k, "w"), indent=1)
s = json.dumps(json.load(open("config/watches/rolex-gmtmaster40-126710blnr.json"))).replace("126710blnr", k)
json.dump(json.loads(s), open("config/watches/rolex-gmtmaster40-%s.json" % k, "w"), indent=1)
j = json.dumps(json.load(open("jobs/rolex-gmtmaster40-126710blnr.json"))).replace("rolex-gmtmaster40-126710blnr", "rolex-gmtmaster40-" + k).replace("rolex-gmt-master-ii-126710blnr", "rolex-gmt-master-ii-" + k)
json.dump(json.loads(j), open("jobs/rolex-gmtmaster40-%s.json" % k, "w"), indent=1)
PY
# 1. re-measure what is listed below in config/watches/rolex-gmtmaster40-$k-measure.json, 2. edit the new spec (model / note / bracelet / emissions), 3. then
python3 -B -m pipeline.rolex_gmtmaster40_prep $k          # trace, dial_photo.jpg, steel_photo.jpg
python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs validate jobs/rolex-gmtmaster40-$k.json   # needs references/$ref/REFERENCE_PLAN.md
python3 -B -m pipeline.jobs run jobs/rolex-gmtmaster40-$k.json --force
cp outputs/jobs/rolex-gmtmaster40-$k/rolex-gmtmaster40-$k.glb models/watches/<glb from rolex_map.json>
```

Edit the new spec's and job's `model` / `note` / `baseline` text by hand (the replace only swaps the SKU / ids; `sources.md` and `REFERENCE_PLAN.md` are per photo). The job's `kit_preset` stays `config/presets/watch_kit_white_front_rolex_gmtmaster40.json` (read-only) while the new photo's pivot (960, 1424) and scale 23.14 px/mm agree within a pixel (check: the alpha mask of the new photo against `rolex-126710blnr_original.png`, a difference below ~60/255 over the head means the case geometry holds; the watch width 1017 px incl. crown, left tip x 497, lug rows 903.5 / 1992.5).

**Same for every GMT-Master II 40 variant (do not touch):** step, prep, kit preset, the case (tip rows, ramp, heights), bezel geometry (rings are re-read from the photo by overlay but are identical on the same camera), 60 flutes, crystal, crown, hub, Cyclops lens (`date` section: heights, rings; lens outline re-picked only if the overlay shows more than 2 px), pose and depth scale, hand heights `hands.z`.

**Per photo (re-measure, never trust these numbers):**
1. `measure.photo` (set by the snippet); then by overlay of `config/watches/rolex-gmtmaster40-<k>-trace.json` on the photo at 3x (hands, lens polygon, ring circles: see how it was done here, all drawn from `measure` / `trace`): pivot, rings, hour / minute / seconds ROIs (`hands.<name>.roi`, thresholds `thr`), the GMT hand `hands.gmt.angle_deg` and `polygons_px` (shaft along the principal axis of the blue / green / other-coloured pixels plus the head triangle; the GMT hand angle and its colour change between models; the head vertices are native px, re-pick them on a 6x crop), `date.lens_px`.
2. Emissions: dial (black 0.28; check plate mean within ~1 %, centre vs rim), steel 0.29, `steel.bezel_emission` 0.265 (re-tune by black / blue / green / brown insert means and the rim / coin means), hands 0.35, `date.emission` 0.26.
3. Bracelet (`strap.bracelet` in the spec): `columns`, `bounds_x_mm` (vertical column lines, top and bottom), `pieces_mm[kind].top / bottom` (joint rows) and the heights `z_<kind>`. **Jubilee (126713GRNR):** columns `outer, inner, centre, inner, outer`; the joint rows must be re-read per photo (the Jubilee links differ in length per row only slightly, check by 4x strip crops: here the thin dark band that precedes each bright plate is the joint, broad black bands inside a plate are reflections); the same layout as 126710BLNR is expected, so compare the strips first and keep these rows if they agree within 2 px. **Oyster (126720VTNR, 126711CHNR, 126710BLRO):** `columns: ["outer", "centre", "outer"]`, `bounds_x_mm` with two boundaries per side, and ONLY the kinds used in `pieces_mm` (`outer`, `centre`; `z_outer` 3.4, `z_centre` 3.5); the Submariner Date 126610LN spec (`config/watches/rolex-submarinerdate41-126610ln.json`, strap.bracelet) is the layout reference for an Oyster bracelet, but its numbers belong to the 22.785 px/mm camera: convert from this photo's scale (23.14 px/mm) and re-measure the rows (the 20 mm Oyster at the lugs of a 40 mm case). **Two-tone / Everose (126711CHNR, 126713GRNR):** the steel map carries the gold (bezel ring, centre links, crown, hands may be gold): steel and bezel emission may need +0.02..0.04 and the polished-rim / centre-link regions must be checked separately; the bracelet layout is as above.
4. `126720VTNR` has the crown on the LEFT: NOT supported by the step / prep (crown, guard rows `crown_rows`, the case-edge anchor `case_edge_left_x` and the silhouette outline assume the crown at +x). Needed: a mirror option (flip the photo in the prep, flip the pose in the step) or a per-side branch; test the GLB export of a mirrored pose before trusting it. Until then build it from a horizontally flipped copy of the photo only if the designer accepts a mirrored result (it would show the crown on the right), otherwise extend the step.
5. `sources.md` row text and the dial / bezel / bracelet description in `REFERENCE_PLAN.md`; if the photo's colours differ from the tracker's variant, say so in the report.
