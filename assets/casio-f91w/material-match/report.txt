CASIO F-91W MATERIAL MATCHING PASS — 2026-10-26

Completed and visually inspected hero.png (2048 x 4438), front.png and
material_closeup.png (2400 x 2400). Each final view rendered once at 512
Cycles samples with adaptive sampling, denoising, neutral AgX and 16-bit PNG.

Diagnosis and changes
The baseline's shared bump/roughness noise and broad uniform highlights made
the strap read as smooth plastic. Its high-contrast look hid lower-strap detail.
Independent molded structure, fine finish and roughness fields now break up
highlights. The strap is slightly rougher than the case. Shader-only edge masks
soften finish transitions without moving vertices. No dirt, scratches or wear.
Case/strap starting grain spacings: 120/140 micrometers; secondary detail:
45/60 micrometers. Effective height bounds: 3.9/4.55 micrometers. These are
look-development choices, not measured factory specifications.

LCD electrodes are darker against a gray-green backing. Crystal thickness and
1.49 IOR remain; roughness is 0.014. Buttons use restrained metal roughness,
without generic bump. Key/fill placement and neutral AgX improve readability.
A hard rectangular reflection from the added lower fill was identified and
removed by repositioning that light outside the crystal reflection path.
100 mm focal lengths retained; hero/front height occupancy is 80%/85%.

Visual validation
Matched baseline/candidate crops use identical cameras, illumination, exposure
and pixel scale. comparison_case.png and comparison_strap.png show stronger
molded surface character and less uniform highlights. comparison_denoising.png
shows the texture survives filtering. One material correction reduced coarse
pocking and blotchy roughness before final rendering. Final full-watch views,
material close-up, and native-resolution hero/button crops were inspected.
The largest improvement is tactile strap response; LCD contrast and lower-strap
readability also improved. Grain is subtle at display size and visible close up.

Preservation and technical validation

Remaining limitations
Material matching is based on processed photographs, not measured surface or
reflectance data. The source's lettering, indicator/segment contours and thin
crystal-edge highlights remain approximate; geometry and graphics were
intentionally preserved. This is an improvement in material credibility, not
a claim of exact photographic equivalence. No new three-quarter render was
requested for this pass; the prior deliverable remains available.

Efficiency
No new research, full rebuild, or additional agent was used. Existing resources
and studio were reused, with grouped background Blender execution through MCP.
Large image inspections and source reads consume context; localized crops and
compact logs limited repetition. Rendering time is reported separately from
token usage; no exact token savings are claimed.
