CASIO F-91W — REFERENCE STUDIO MATCH

Scene-only treatment of the validated material-match project. Original watch
geometry, materials, graphics, dimensions and strap construction are preserved.
The original source and its script/report/hero are retained in source-snapshot/.

Changes
- Level, straight-on 100 mm hero camera; 2400 x 2872 output. Projected product
  bounds occupy 62% of frame height, top 9%, bottom 71%, horizontally centered.
- Narrow overhead/front area light with restricted spread creates a central
  vertical band. Its power was reduced after spread concentration made the
  initial test too bright. Existing feathered reflection panel was narrowed,
  repositioned and hidden from camera rays while retaining reflection visibility.
- Low frontal fill supports the LCD; restrained left/right strips define edges.
  The prior lower-strap fill is disabled. World illumination is zero.
- Black ground lowered 0.5 mm, giving 0.7 mm clearance below the lowest watch
  geometry. Ground roughness is 0.15–0.21 with a dark foreground transition.
- Grazing horizontal ground strip produces the soft glow. A narrow red area
  emitter at the lower front lights the strap underside with restrained spill.
- AgX neutral look retained; exposure -0.65. No haze, bloom or painted reflections.

Reference limitations
The reference depicts a wider metal chronograph. The Casio keeps its narrower
silhouette, LCD, colored printing and softer resin reflections. The reference's
fine longitudinal strap streaks are not reproduced by altering Casio materials.
The floor glow is slightly lower and more diffuse, and the reflected strap is
less distinct. These are remaining differences, not an exact pixel match.
The small 402 x 481 reference limits inference about the original light rig.

Execution
One agent; no new research or builders. MCP protocol 15/add-on 1.8 compatibility
and Python communication verified. All work used isolated Blender 5.2.3 LTS
processes; the unrelated live three-object scene was preserved.
Five small lighting previews bracketed spread, intensity, accent aiming and
floor response. Native inspection of the 512-sample final revealed faint LCD
mottling, prompting a sampling-only cleanup at 1536 samples, adaptive threshold
0.0007. No product shaders were changed to hide noise.

Final inspection and validation
The 1536-sample result and native face, strap and floor crops were inspected.
LCD mottling is visibly reduced relative to the 512-sample output. The crystal
has no distracting hard rectangular reflection, resin grain remains visible
under the central highlight, and the red underside accent stays very faint.
Reference comparison is generated from the delivered hero, without exposure
or contrast adjustment. Full-resolution 16-bit output remains available.
Reopened-file fingerprint validation passed for 120 manufactured components
and their product shader networks. Case bounds: 35.2 x 38.2 x 8.5 mm. Embedded
fonts remain packed. Original source file hashes remain unchanged.

Measured rendering time
Initial 512-sample final: 176.77 seconds (2m 57s).
Delivered 1536-sample cleanup: 574.70 seconds (9m 35s).
Combined full-resolution rendering: 751.47 seconds (12m 31s), excluding previews.
No exact token-savings claim is made.
